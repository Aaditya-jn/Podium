# Brag Plan: Podium

## What is this app?
Podium helps students and professionals build speaking and writing habits with fresh prompts, practice sessions, and AI feedback.

## The angle
Confidence grows through small reps. The film shows one low-pressure practice loop end to end: choose a topic, speak for a moment, then leave with a clear next step.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“Find your voice.” arrives over Podium’s warm paper-and-coral identity, then resolves to the real site line, “Then make it heard.” The hook is personal and direct, not a claim of instant confidence.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Topic setup: category, Speak/Write, and difficulty choices lead to a timely prompt.
- Practice: a live transcript, timer, and pace make the session feel approachable and measurable.
- Review: score breakdown and specific written feedback make progress tangible.

## Outro / punchline
“One thought at a time.” Podium mark and a concise invitation: “Practice with Podium.”

## User flow worth showing
Choose a topic and mode → practice speaking with a visible timer and transcript → see a score and AI writing feedback.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm, premium product film with human pacing
- Interpretation: restrained editorial typography, tactile UI details, confident motion, and enough hold time to read each screen.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, steady support for a human-centered product story.
- Music: a 20-second edit of `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, instrumental, mid-energy.
- Music treatment: start at 0s, about 0.28 volume, gentle opening, soften into the final logo and fade during the last 0.7s.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, estimated 109.96 BPM. Target the topic reveal near 8.74s and the review reveal near 17.47s; align only subtle card details to the beat grid.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle. Let the orbit artwork and warm halo breathe slightly with the music; no waveform graphics or aggressive pulsing.
- SFX posture: sparse, soft button and card accents at actual UI interactions.
- Audio-coupled moments: mode selection and topic reveal; transcript typing; score reveal.
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no loud impacts, and no sound that implies an unverified action.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Find your voice — 4s
Warm paper canvas, small Podium mark, orbit-and-wave artwork. “Find your voice.” then the supporting line “Then make it heard.” The composition references the exact landing-page hero wording. Headline holds clearly before the scene handoff.
Sequential/interaction: brand mark arrives, then headline, then support line.
Audio intent: a gentle opening and one soft logo accent.
Audio-coupled idea: headline resolves on a downbeat, with a very subtle UI tap.
Transition mood: soft editorial slide → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — Choose a prompt — 5s
Recreated setup UI based on Podium: Everyday life / Career & workplace / Technology & AI category tiles, Speak selected, Medium difficulty, and the prompt “What makes a good teammate?” A “Classic” label accurately identifies the curated example.
Sequential/interaction: show category tiles, select Speak, then reveal the prompt.
Audio intent: calm momentum and a tactile selection sound.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft click on mode selection and a light card landing on prompt reveal.
Transition mood: warm blur crossfade → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — Practice in the moment — 5.5s
Actual-flow recreation: a focused practice card with the topic, timer at 00:42, transcript text, 38 words, and 132 wpm. The sample transcript reads: “A good teammate listens carefully, shares context, and makes it easier for everyone to do their best work.” Keep the live indicators legible and avoid implying that these sample metrics belong to a real user.
Sequential/interaction: a cursor begins the session; the sentence types in, then timer and pace settle.
Audio intent: instrumental bed carries the cadence; restrained key taps imply writing/speech transcript.
Audio-coupled idea: typed sentence and small stat arrival, timed loosely around beats.
Transition mood: clean push slide → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — Leave with a next step — 5.5s
Review card shows score 86/100, clear length/vocabulary/clarity/pace bars, and grounded AI feedback: “Clear opening and useful example.” / “Try a shorter closing sentence.” Then the Podium mark and the exact line “One thought at a time.” End card holds for readability.
Sequential/interaction: score appears, two feedback lines arrive, then brand and final invitation.
Audio intent: reassuring resolve with a small, warm score accent and gentle fade.
Audio-coupled idea: score reveal near the 17.47s cue; logo holds while music fades.
Transition mood: soft crossfade into the final brand lockup.
