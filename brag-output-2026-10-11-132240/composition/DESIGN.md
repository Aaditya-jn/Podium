# Podium Launch Film Design

## Style Prompt
Warm editorial product film rooted in Podium’s actual interface. Human, composed, and encouraging. Use a tactile paper canvas, ink text, restrained coral emphasis, and olive status accents. Treat each practice screen as a real place someone can use; let confident copy and clear UI carry the story.

## Colors
- Warm paper background: `#faf8f3`
- Warm card: `#fffefa`
- Primary ink: `#25211e`
- Coral accent: `#eb775f`
- Olive detail: `#8d9951`
- Supporting line: `#857e76`

## Typography
- Display: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif.
- UI and body: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif.
- Headlines are large and expressive; UI labels use clear, high-contrast sans-serif.

## Motion
Soft editorial slide and blur-crossfade transitions. Gentle orbital movement, deliberate UI selections, and concise entrances that leave text settled long enough to read.

## What NOT to Do
- No neon gradients, generic SaaS stock imagery, or abstract filler.
- No fake testimonials, fabricated personal records, or exaggerated performance claims.
- No rapid flashing text, aggressive zooms, or loud sound effects.
- No claim that optional camera or AI insights measure emotion or personality.
